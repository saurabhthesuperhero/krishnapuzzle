Privacy Policy

Krishna Puzzle Adventure (Android game)

Last updated: 13 October 2026

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-in. We never ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • We do not collect personal data. Your game progress and settings stay on your device.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device information to show and measure ads, as explained below.
  • Purchases are optional and handled entirely by Google Play.
  • Made for families. Ads are requested as child-directed and are never personalised.

Who we are

Krishna Puzzle Adventure (“the game”, “we”, “us”) is a story puzzle game for Android published by the developer listed on its Google Play page. If you have any question about this policy, write to superprogrammersaurabh@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The game has no login and no servers of its own. Everything the game needs to remember is saved only on your device:

  • level progress, stars, makhan (coins), hints and unlocked items;
  • settings such as language (English / हिंदी), music, sound, vibration and reminders;
  • Goshala, album, achievement and daily-reward progress.

This data never leaves your device through us. If Android backup is switched on for your Google account, Android may include it in your encrypted device backup, which is handled by Google under its own privacy policy. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads (banners, occasional full-screen ads between levels and optional “watch a video” rewards) using the Google Mobile Ads SDK by Google LLC. To deliver ads, measure them, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud, the SDK may automatically collect and process:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general (city-level) location;
  • device and app information such as device model, operating system and version, language and the game version;
  • ad interactions such as impressions and taps, plus diagnostic and crash data from the ads SDK;
  • device identifiers, only where the platform makes them available to the SDK.

This data is collected and used by Google, not by us. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the AdMob data disclosure.

Your choices

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and some US states), the game shows Google’s consent message before any ad is requested. You can review or change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the game.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device).
  • Rewarded videos are always optional. Buying “Remove Ads” turns off banners and full-screen ads.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (for example Remove Ads, the VIP pass and makhan packs) are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details; Google Play only tells the game which item was bought so it can be unlocked. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Notifications and sharing

  • Reminders (for example “your cows are hungry” or a new daily challenge) are optional local notifications scheduled on your device. No notification server is involved and you can turn them off in the game’s settings or in Android settings.
  • Sharing a story card or score uses Android’s standard share sheet. The game does not see what you share or with whom.
  • Rate us opens Google Play’s own review dialog.

Children

Krishna Puzzle Adventure is made to be enjoyed by families, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in their country). Because children play the game:

  • every ad request is tagged for child-directed treatment (COPPA) and for users under the age of consent (GDPR);
  • personalised advertising and remarketing are turned off, and ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences (G);
  • the game does not access the Android advertising ID;
  • only ad SDKs that are part of Google Play’s Families self-certified ads program are used.

Parents or guardians who have a question, or who believe information about their child was collected, can contact us at superprogrammersaurabh@gmail.com and we will help right away.

Data retention, deletion and your rights

Because we do not hold any personal data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. Game data on your device is removed when you clear the game’s storage or uninstall it. For data processed by Google (ads, purchases, backup) you can use the controls in your Google Account (Data & privacy). Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data; contact us and we will answer every request.

Security

The game communicates with Google services over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and requests only the permissions it needs: internet and network-state access for ads and purchases, vibration for haptic feedback, notifications for optional reminders, and "run at startup" so those reminders are scheduled again after the phone restarts.

Changes to this policy

If the game or this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the game’s release notes.
